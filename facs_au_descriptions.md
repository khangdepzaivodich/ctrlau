# Facial Action Coding System (FACS) - Action Units (AUs)

The Facial Action Coding System (FACS) breaks down facial expressions into Action Units (AUs), which correspond to the contraction or relaxation of specific facial muscles. AUs describe the physical mechanics of the face rather than subjective emotions. Each AU is typically measured on a 5-level intensity scale from A (trace) to E (maximum).

## Main Action Units

| AU | Name | Underlying Muscle(s) | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AU 1** | Inner Brow Raiser | Frontalis (pars medialis) | Pulls the inner corners of the eyebrows upwards. |
| **AU 2** | Outer Brow Raiser | Frontalis (pars lateralis) | Pulls the outer corners of the eyebrows upwards. |
| **AU 4** | Brow Lowerer | Corrugator supercilii, Depressor supercilii | Lowers and draws the eyebrows together. |
| **AU 5** | Upper Lid Raiser | Levator palpebrae superioris | Raises the upper eyelid, widening the eye. |
| **AU 6** | Cheek Raiser | Orbicularis oculi (pars orbitalis) | Raises the cheeks, causing crow's feet wrinkles around the eyes. |
| **AU 7** | Lid Tightener | Orbicularis oculi (pars palpebralis) | Tightens the eyelids, narrowing the eye aperture. |
| **AU 9** | Nose Wrinkler | Levator labii superioris alaquae nasi | Wrinkles the nose, often raising the upper lip. |
| **AU 10** | Upper Lip Raiser | Levator labii superioris | Raises the upper lip directly upwards. |
| **AU 12** | Lip Corner Puller | Zygomaticus major | Pulls the corners of the lips upwards and outwards (smile). |
| **AU 15** | Lip Corner Depressor | Depressor anguli oris | Pulls the corners of the lips downwards. |
| **AU 17** | Chin Raiser | Mentalis | Pushes the chin upwards, wrinkling chin skin and pushing up the lower lip. |
| **AU 23** | Lip Tightener | Orbicularis oris | Tightens and narrows the lips. |
| **AU 24** | Lip Presser | Orbicularis oris | Presses the lips together. |
| **AU 25** | Lips Part | Depressor labii inferioris, or relaxation | Parts the lips, separating them slightly. |
| **AU 26** | Jaw Drop | Masseter (relaxed), Temporalis (relaxed) | Drops the lower jaw, parting the teeth. |

## Additional Categories

*   **Head Movement Codes:** For example, AU 51 (Head turn left), AU 53 (Head up).
*   **Eye Movement Codes:** For example, AU 61 (Eyes turn left), AU 63 (Eyes up).
*   **Action Descriptors (ADs):** Movements that do not have a specific underlying muscle action, such as "head forward" or "eyes up".
